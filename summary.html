Run Summary & Architecture

Human Strategy & AI Assistance

The core optimization strategies—diagnosing the Devanagari token bloat, engineering the learning rate curve, and conducting iterative capacity vs. convergence testing—were entirely human-led. AI coding assistants were utilized as syntax accelerators to draft standard boilerplate (e.g., the AdamW loop, Cosine Annealing math, and the BPE script structure), allowing me to focus exclusively on high-level architectural debugging and hyperparameter balancing.

Key Upgrades & Final Architecture

  • Engine: Diagnosed the inefficiency of the baseline's constant LR. Engineered a custom Cosine Annealing schedule (100-step warmup, peak LR 1e-3) paired with AdamW and gradient clipping to force aggressive but stable convergence within the hard step limit.
  • Tokenizer: Identified that 3-byte Devanagari characters were exhausting the context window. Designed and trained a custom BPE tokenizer (vocab limit: 512) to effectively compress the Hindi text while strictly maintaining the required lossless byte-level fallback.
  • Architecture: Conducted rigorous empirical testing on parameter allocation. Concluded that a highly regularized, compact parameter configuration (4 layers, 4 heads, 160 embd, tie_weights=False | 1.42M params) vastly outperforms larger 1.9M+ parameter architectures that suffer from severe underfitting under the strict 2,000-step constraint.
