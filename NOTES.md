My best configuration utilizes a custom Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) tokenizer with a vocabulary size of 512, which efficiently compresses the Hindi text in the corpus compared to the baseline byte-level tokenizer. For the training engine, I replaced the basic Adam optimizer with AdamW to introduce weight decay regularization. I also implemented a Cosine Annealing learning rate schedule with a 100-step linear warmup, boosting the peak learning rate to 1e-3, alongside gradient clipping to maintain stability. Architecturally, I retained the baseline 4-layer, 4-head, 160-embedding structure (1.42M parameters), as my logs proved that pushing closer to the 2M cap required more than 2,000 steps to converge properly. This combination maximizes learning and context compression within the strict 2,000-step budget.
